# Entrance wash [trials 75–87]
- Nothing in the reference enters at full colour: each new layer is washed toward white for 1–3 frames. The flash belongs to the layer, not the frame.
- Default: 2 frames. Thin layers: 3 frames at 0.9 / 0.65 / 0.35 (washes right on stills didn't read at speed on thin layers). [87]
- Do it when the layer is built. Rolling it out afterwards cost 13 trials.
- Answer questions about where washes are from the log, not memory (a wrong "nothing after 0:46 has a wash" was given once).
