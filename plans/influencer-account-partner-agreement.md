## 1. Purpose
The Creator produces AI-generated content featuring the AI influencer **"____________"** (the **Character**). The Partner holds and/or manages the TikTok account **@____________** (the **Account**) that publishes this content.

## 2. Roles
**Creator will:**
- Create and supply the Character's videos and images.
- Decide the Character's look, personality, and content direction.
- Find and negotiate brand deals (unless agreed otherwise in writing).

**Partner will:**
- Register and hold the Account in their **own legal name**, with their own ID, tax details, and bank account (required for platform payouts).
- Post content on the agreed schedule: ____ posts per week.
- Reply to comments and DMs in the Character's voice, following the Creator's guidelines.
- Not post content the Creator hasn't approved.

## 3. Ownership
- The **Character** (name, look, personality, images, trained AI models/LoRAs, and all content made by the Creator) **belongs to the Creator**.
- The Partner gets a licence to use the Character **only on the Account**, for as long as this agreement lasts.
- Account login details are shared with the Creator. The Partner will not change them without telling the Creator within 24 hours.

- **Costs** (GPU, tools, ads) are paid by: ____________ (or deducted before the split).
- **Payment timing:** the Partner sends the Creator's share within **__ days** of receiving funds, via ____________ (Wise / Payoneer / crypto).
- **Transparency:** the Partner shares payout screenshots/statements each month.
- **Taxes:** each party is responsible for their own taxes in their own country.

## 5. Rules both sides follow
- All realistic AI content is **labelled as AI-generated** on TikTok.
- All paid promotions are **clearly disclosed** (#ad + TikTok's Branded Content toggle).
- No VPNs, false details, bought followers, or anything breaking TikTok's terms.
- No real person's face or likeness is used without written consent.

## 6. Confidentiality
The Partner keeps the Creator's production methods, prompts, tools, brand contacts, and earnings private, during the agreement and for **__ months** after it ends.

## 7. Ending the agreement
- Either party can end it with **__ days' written notice** (a message is fine).
- **Immediate termination** is allowed for: breaking Section 5, withholding money, or taking over the Account.
- **On termination:**
  - [ ] **Option A:** the Account is handed to the Creator or a person they choose (logins, email, phone).
  - [ ] **Option B:** the Account is deleted or stops using the Character within **7 days**.
  - Money earned before termination is split as normal, even if paid out later.
- The Partner may **not** continue using the Character, or a lookalike, after termination.
